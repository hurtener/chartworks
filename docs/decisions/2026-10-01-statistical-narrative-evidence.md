# Statistical narrative continuation

### D-092 — Closed retained statistical narrative evidence

Status: implemented for qualification

The frozen narrative consumer needs exact trend, extrema and variance statements
without granting a provider arithmetic, source-read or free-form claim authority.

New reviewed outputs may opt into `bounded-narrative-v3`. Its closed declarations
bind one series and explicit temporal meaning to exact schema coordinates. The
service calculates bounded retained-prefix evidence and the provider selects only
matching evidence IDs. Population variance retains an exact rational; direction,
ties, NULL exclusions, reduction and truncation are deterministic evidence.

Existing output selection, source authorization, sensitivity, amount disclosure,
budget reservations and PostgreSQL retained-result recomputation apply. A
required excluded field makes the narrative unavailable before provider work.
Old policies remain valid with their historical text/evidence bytes. This adds
no SQL operation, model role, unrestricted formula, database migration or resource
limit. Static HTML additionally preserves escaped narrative caveats that its
previous renderer omitted; immutable stored renditions are not rewritten.

See [the statistical contract](../contracts/statistical-narratives-v3.md) for the
supported domain and qualification boundaries. Current live-provider quality and
isolated renderer deployment remain separate gates.
