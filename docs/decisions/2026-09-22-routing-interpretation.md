### D-076 — Topic choice and semantic spans are versioned, replayable route evidence

Status: accepted for CW-07, 2026-09-22.

Chartworks owns deterministic topic choice when callers omit a topic. Candidate
publications come only from the signed-reachable current catalog and current source
contracts. A versioned evidence policy aggregates complementary facet signals and
optional authorized reranking into explicit no-route, clarification or selected
outcomes. Raw vector distance is retained as one input and is not labeled calibrated
query correctness.

Reviewed non-sensitive values, geography vocabulary and temporal policies feed one
deterministic interpretation stage. Its locale/parser/anchor, publication and source
pins, canonical values and typed spans are sealed into route evidence and replayed
under current authority. Ambiguity fails closed. Corrections can remove an inferred
target or select another reviewed value; arbitrary literals cannot become filters.

Interpretation supplies mandatory model context and closed business constraints,
but grants no authority and issues no plan. The existing validator and read-only
execution boundary remain the only SQL execution path.

Why: routing quality depends on inspectable topic and span decisions. Preserving
those decisions before generation prevents the model from repeatedly rediscovering
governed values and dates while keeping authority, privacy and SQL safety unchanged.
